A Refuge in the Valley

Hidden in a deep valley beneath the western feet of the Misty Mountains lay one of the last great refuges of the Elves in Middle-earth.

Men and Hobbits called it Rivendell.

The Elves named it Imladris.

Waterfalls descended from the heights around it. The river Bruinen, the Loudwater, guarded its approaches, while wooded slopes concealed the valley from travelers who did not know the way. Within stood the house of Elrond Half-elven, where songs and stories preserved memories reaching back thousands of years.

Yet Rivendell had not been founded merely as a place of beauty.

It was born in war.

The War of the Elves and Sauron

In the middle of the Second Age, Sauron deceived the Elven-smiths of Eregion and helped them attain great skill in the making of Rings of Power.

But when Sauron secretly forged the One Ring in Orodruin, the Elves perceived his betrayal.

War followed.

In Second Age 1695, Sauron invaded Eriador. Gil-galad, High King of the Noldor in Lindon, sent a force commanded by Elrond to aid Eregion.

They came too late to save it.

In 1697, Eregion was overrun. Its lord, Celebrimbor, was captured and slain, and Sauron's armies drove across the country.

Elrond gathered those survivors he could and retreated northward.

There, in a hidden valley beneath the mountains, he established a stronghold.

That refuge was Imladris.

The First Siege of Rivendell

Sauron did not leave the new refuge unchallenged.

His forces pressed against Elrond, and Rivendell was besieged. Yet the valley endured while Sauron's armies continued westward against Lindon.

Then aid came from across the Sea.

The Númenórean king Tar-Minastir sent a great fleet to Middle-earth. Joined with the forces of Gil-galad, the Númenóreans shattered Sauron's armies.

By Second Age 1701, Sauron had been driven out of Eriador.

Rivendell survived.

At a council held afterward, the Elves determined that Imladris should remain as an eastern stronghold. Gil-galad entrusted Elrond with Vilya, the Ring of Air, greatest of the Three Rings of the Elves.

Its power would later help preserve Rivendell against the weariness and decay of the passing ages.

Elrond Half-elven

The master of Rivendell belonged to many histories.

Elrond was descended from both Elves and Men. His parents were Eärendil and Elwing, and through his ancestry ran the blood of the great houses of both peoples.

At the end of the First Age, Elrond and his brother Elros were permitted to choose to which kindred they would belong.

Elrond chose the Elves.

Elros chose the fate of Men and became the first King of Númenor.

Thus, thousands of years later, the rulers of the Dúnedain were distant descendants of Elrond's own brother.

That ancient kinship would become deeply important to Rivendell.

The Last Alliance

Near the end of the Second Age, Sauron again threatened the Free Peoples.

In Second Age 3430, the Last Alliance of Elves and Men was formed by Gil-galad and Elendil.

Their armies gathered before marching east.

Rivendell became one of the places bound closely to that alliance, and Elrond went to war as the herald of Gil-galad.

The campaign ended before the gates of Barad-dûr. Gil-galad and Elendil were slain, but Sauron was overthrown when Isildur cut the One Ring from his hand.

Elrond urged Isildur to destroy the Ring.

Isildur refused.

The consequences of that decision would reach Rivendell more than three thousand years later.

The Heirs of Isildur

In the Third Age, the northern kingdom of Arnor was divided and eventually destroyed.

But the line of Isildur did not end.

After the fall of Arthedain, the surviving Dúnedain became a wandering people, and their chieftains maintained the royal line in secret.

Rivendell became their sanctuary.

The heirs of Isildur were fostered there, while heirlooms of the House of Isildur were kept in Elrond's care. Among them were the Shards of Narsil, the broken sword with which Isildur had cut the Ring from Sauron's hand.

In this way, Rivendell became something more than an Elven refuge.

It became a treasury of the lost North-kingdom and a guardian of its memory.

Aragorn

In Third Age 2931, Aragorn, son of Arathorn II, was born.

His father was killed when Aragorn was only two years old. His mother, Gilraen, brought him to Rivendell, where Elrond received him into his household.

His identity was concealed for his protection.

He was called Estel, meaning Hope.

Aragorn grew to manhood in Rivendell without knowing that he was the heir of Isildur. When he reached the age of twenty, Elrond revealed his ancestry and gave him the heirlooms of his house.

Soon afterward Aragorn met Arwen, Elrond's daughter, who had returned to Rivendell after spending many years in Lothlórien.

Their love would bind together once more the ancient lines of Elves and Men.

But Elrond placed a hard condition upon their marriage: Arwen could wed no lesser Man than the King of both Gondor and Arnor.

Aragorn therefore left Rivendell to begin the long years of wandering that would eventually lead him to the throne.

Bilbo Baggins Comes to Rivendell

Not all who entered the hidden valley came bearing the burdens of kings.

In Third Age 2941, a company of thirteen Dwarves arrived with Gandalf and a bewildered Hobbit named Bilbo Baggins.

They were traveling east on the quest of Thorin Oakenshield to reclaim Erebor from the dragon Smaug.

At Rivendell, Elrond examined the swords recovered from a Troll-hoard and identified them as ancient blades of Gondolin. He also discovered moon-letters upon Thorin's map, revealing secret instructions concerning the hidden entrance to the Lonely Mountain.

Bilbo never forgot Rivendell.

Many years later, when age and weariness had begun to settle upon him, he returned there.

This time, he stayed.

The White Council

Rivendell also stood near the center of the long struggle against the returning Shadow.

During the Third Age, Elrond, Gandalf, Galadriel, Saruman, and other leaders of the Wise took counsel concerning the growing power of the Necromancer in Dol Guldur.

This body became known as the White Council.

Elrond's knowledge of the Second Age, the Rings of Power, and the history of Sauron made Rivendell one of the great repositories of wisdom in Middle-earth.

Its defense was therefore not merely a matter of walls, rivers, or hidden paths.

Knowledge itself was one of Rivendell's weapons.

The Ring-bearer

In 3018, the ancient history of Rivendell and the fate of the One Ring finally converged.

Frodo Baggins, carrying Sauron's Ring, fled eastward from the Shire.

At Weathertop he was wounded by the Witch-king with a Morgul-knife. Gravely ill, he continued toward Rivendell while the Nazgûl pursued him.

At the Ford of Bruinen, the Nine nearly overtook him.

But the river rose against them.

A great flood swept through the ford, carrying away the horses of the Ringwraiths and breaking their immediate power.

Frodo was brought safely into Rivendell, where Elrond healed the wound as far as it could be healed.

And there, at last, the One Ring entered the house of the Elf who had witnessed Isildur claim it more than three thousand years before.

The Council of Elrond

On 25 October 3018, representatives of many of the Free Peoples gathered in Rivendell.

Some had come seeking answers to their own troubles. Instead, their separate stories were discovered to be parts of the same great danger.

At the Council of Elrond, the history of the Ring was recounted.

Many possibilities were considered.

It could not safely be hidden. It could not be entrusted to another power. Nor could the Free Peoples hope to wield it against Sauron without becoming corrupted themselves.

Only one course remained.

The Ring had to be taken into Mordor and destroyed in the fire in which it had been made.

Frodo volunteered to carry it.

From those gathered in Rivendell, the Fellowship of the Ring was formed: Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Aragorn, Boromir, Legolas, Gimli, and Gandalf.

On 25 December 3018, they departed the valley.

The road to Mount Doom had begun.

The Sword Reforged

Before the Fellowship departed, another relic preserved in Rivendell returned to history.

The broken sword Narsil was reforged by Elven-smiths.

Aragorn renamed it Andúril, Flame of the West.

For generations the shattered blade had represented a kingdom lost and a kingship waiting to be restored.

Now its heir carried it south.

Rivendell had guarded the memory of Arnor.

At last, that memory was leaving the valley to become a kingdom once more.

The Passing of Rivendell

Sauron was defeated in 3019, and Aragorn became King Elessar of the Reunited Kingdom.

He married Arwen, daughter of Elrond.

But victory brought an ending as well as a beginning.

The One Ring had been destroyed, and with it the power of the Three Rings faded. The preservation that had sheltered places such as Rivendell could not endure unchanged.

In Third Age 3021, Elrond left Rivendell and journeyed west with Galadriel, Gandalf, Frodo, and Bilbo.

They passed over the Sea.

Elrond's sons, Elladan and Elrohir, remained in Rivendell for a time, and other members of his household may have remained with them. But the great days of Imladris were ending.

The dominion of Men had begun.

The Last Homely House

For more than four thousand years, Rivendell had endured.

It began as a refuge for survivors fleeing the armies of Sauron.

It became a fortress against him.

Then a sanctuary for the heirs of a fallen kingdom.

A treasury of memory.

A meeting place of the Wise.

A home for Bilbo.

A refuge for Frodo.

And finally the place where representatives of Elves, Dwarves, Men, and Hobbits agreed upon the desperate course that would bring about Sauron's final defeat.

Other strongholds were greater in arms. Other Elven realms were broader and more splendid.

But Rivendell possessed a different strength.

Within its hidden valley, the past was remembered without entirely shutting out the future.

For an age, weary travelers came down from the wild and found lights among the trees, voices raised in song, good food upon the table, and wisdom waiting beside the fire.

And then, when their strength had returned, they went out again.
